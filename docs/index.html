    ADGM via PR Newswire

    ADGM deployed AI across licensing, supervision and customer service; about 25% of customer enquiries are now resolved instantly, and it will invest more than AED 400m in AI, data and cyber capabilities to 2029.

    Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone passes 1,000 AI-related companies

    WAM

    More than 1,000 AI-related companies now operate in SPC Free Zone, which also runs an AI-powered licensing system; company formation packages start from AED 5,750.

  5. 28 Jul
    DIFCProgrammes & Ecosystem

    DIFC: AI, FinTech and innovation firms up 39% to 1,933 in H1 2026

    DIFC Newsroom

    DIFC's H1 2026 results: the Innovation Hub added 361 companies, taking AI, FinTech and innovation firms to 1,933; DIFC restated its AI-Native centre plan (USD 3.5bn value, 25,000 jobs).

  6. 6 Jul
    Innovation City (RAK)Product & Launch

    Innovation City (RAK) and Siada launch the Middle East's first sovereign AI data centre with NVIDIA B200 GPUs

    Innovation City Newsroom · with Siada (IOPn), NVIDIA

    Licensed companies can rent sovereign GPU compute by the hour, reserve capacity or run fully managed environments, with data kept in the UAE.

  7. 7 May
    KEZADPartnership / MoU

    ROX signs lease for one of the Middle East's first advanced AI manufacturing centres in KEZAD Musaffah

    KEZAD Group Newsroom · with ROX, ADIO

    A 10,000 sqm intelligent manufacturing facility starting operations in H2 2026, targeting 300,000 vehicles a year by 2030.

  8. 23 Apr
    Dubai Silicon OasisDubai CommerCityInvestment & Funding

    DIEZ and VOLT UAE to build AI-ready data centre ('AI factory') in Dubai Silicon Oasis

    Government of Dubai Media Office · with VOLT UAE, Schneider Electric

    Joint venture for a facility of up to 60,000 sqm: an initial 29 MW followed by 100 MW of committed power. DIEZ provides land and core infrastructure; VOLT develops, finances and operates; Schneider Electric supplies power systems.

  9. 21 Apr
    DIFCProgrammes & Ecosystem

    DIFC to become the world's first AI-Native financial centre

    Government of Dubai Media Office

    AI to be embedded across DIFC's legal and regulatory frameworks, business operations, talent systems, infrastructure and physical district, with a stated USD 3.5bn (AED 12.9bn) economic benefit and 25,000 jobs; plans include a full-stack AI Campus (regulation, training, compute, physical AI) and robotics and digital twins across the district by 2030.

  10. 18 Feb
    Dubai CommerCityPartnership / MoU

    IIT Madras Global to establish its first Applied AI Innovation Centre at Dubai CommerCity

    Press Information Bureau (India) · with IIT Madras Global Research Foundation, MCA Gulf

    MoU signed at WORLDEF Dubai (12-14 Feb). The centre's first phase hosts six applied-AI start-ups with a combined valuation of about USD 118m, targeting smart cities, energy, logistics and construction.
