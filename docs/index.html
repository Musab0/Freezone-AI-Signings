AI is now a whole-of-zone strategy for the two leaders

DIFC targets USD 3.5bn in economic value and 25,000 jobs from becoming AI-native. ADGM has AI live across licensing, supervision and customer service, backed by an AED 400m roadmap to 2029.

What it means for DMCC

Recommended responses for discussion.

1

Name a flagship AI proposition for trade and commodities

Turn the Future of Trade finding (AI drives 43% of trade growth) into a member-facing programme with measurable targets, as DIFC and ADGM have done.

2

Publish DMCC's own AI adoption results

ADGM now quotes 25 AI-enabled functions and 5,000 hours saved. Equivalent metrics for DMCC member services would neutralise that comparison.

3

Offer members access to AI compute through partners

Innovation City shows that GPU access is a differentiator. A partnership for GPU credits or sovereign compute would cost little and match the move.

4

Lead on AI governance from the AI & Agentics Council

DMCC's council with Deloitte is a unique asset. Pair it with a talent programme with a public target to match peers' skills numbers.
